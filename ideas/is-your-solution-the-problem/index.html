Is Your Solution
the Problem?

A working article space for one of the questions I keep returning to: what happens when we become more attached to the solution than the system around the problem?

This article page is ready for the full essay. Existing LinkedIn writing can be brought here as the initial archive, while future essays can be published directly on the site.

The question

Founders are often told to fall in love with the problem, not the solution. It is good advice, but reality is more complicated. Products, customers, incentives, distribution, timing and operating constraints interact. A solution can be technically sound and still create a new problem elsewhere in the system.

What I want to explore

This series looks at business questions through a systems lens: where assumptions hide, how one decision changes another part of the business, and why solving the visible problem is not always the same as improving the system.
